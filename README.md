# Sanketam (సంకేతం / संकेतम्)

> **"Stay close to family, without losing your independence."**

A privacy-first family connection platform built for young Indians living away from their parents — in Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, hostels, their first jobs, or abroad — and the parents who worry quietly every day.

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Fdathasaiswaroopgudimella-png%2FSANKETAM)

---

## The Real Problem

* **Parents worry quietly and constantly:** *"Did my child eat? Did they reach home? Are they okay?"* — with no easy, non-intrusive way to know.
* **Young people feel pressured:** Constant check-in calls and messages start to feel like surveillance and micro-management rather than care.
* **WhatsApp carries messages, not routines:** It has no healthy system for balancing care with independence.
* **Location trackers (Life360/FindMy) fail trust:** Tracking live GPS, battery percentages, and driving speed triggers resentment, rebelliousness, and circumvention.

---

## How Sanketam Works

```
1. 20-Second Voice Note
   Young person records a quick note: "Today was busy, I reached home, I'm okay."
               │
               ▼
2. AI Tone Softening & Cultural Translation
   AI turns it into a warm, respectful update in the parent's native language
   (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, etc.).
               │
               ▼
3. Non-Negotiable Consent Gate
   User chooses what to share: Quick Safe Ping, Dinner & Routine, or Full Warm Note.
   Nothing is ever sent without the user's manual approval.
               │
               ▼
4. Parent Reassurance
   Parents receive a calm, soothing update via WhatsApp or the Accessible Senior Portal
   with 1-tap Indic voice playback — no repeated calling required.
```

---

## The Non-Negotiable Rule

> **We never auto-detect mood. We never secretly alert parents. No location tracking. No reading private chats.**
> 
> The moment an app quietly reads emotion or notifies a parent without the user's explicit choice, it becomes surveillance — and it destroys the trust the product is built on. **The young person controls every single update.**

## Key Features

### 🎒 Youth & Teenager Experience
* **15–20s Voice Recording:** Real microphone input with an animated waveform canvas.
* **3 Granular Disclosure Levels:**
  1. *Quick Safe Ping:* Reassurance only, zero extra details.
  2. *Dinner & Routine:* Arrival confirmation, meal status, and call schedule.
  3. *Warm Reflection:* Work highlights, filial warmth, and weekend call planning.
* **7 Indic Languages:** Native translation into Telugu (తెలుగు), Hindi (हिन्दी), Tamil (தமிழ்), Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Marathi (मराठी), Bengali (বাংলা), and English.
* **100% Consent Approval Gate:** Preview of the softened translation before one-tap dispatch.

### 👵 Senior Parent Portal & WhatsApp View
* **Accessible Senior Interface:** Extra-large typography, high-contrast layouts, and clean status indicators (PG Safe, Dinner Done, Resting Well).
* **1-Tap Voice Narration:** Browser-synthesized speech audio in regional Indian accents so elderly parents can *listen* to updates effortlessly.
* **WhatsApp Business Simulation:** Realistic WhatsApp message bubble with audio waveform bar, double blue ticks, and one-tap blessing replies.
* **Filial Feedback:** Parents can send instant 1-tap blessings back to the child (*"క్షేమంగా ఉండు కన్నా ❤️"*, *"Take rest beta 🙏"*, *"Ate your dinner? 🍲"*).

### 📊 Concept & Pitch Deck Integration
* Interactive 12-slide viewer directly inside the application.
* Full-resolution slide renders with keyboard navigation (← and →) and thumbnail selection.
